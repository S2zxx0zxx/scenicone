ScenicOne Furniture

ScenicOne Furniture is a luxury furniture manufacturer based in Jodhpur, Rajasthan, India — India's furniture capital. Founded in 1985, ScenicOne crafts premium solid wood furniture using traditional Rajasthani woodworking techniques. Our pieces are exported worldwide, serving homeowners, interior designers, hoteliers, and hospitality businesses in the USA, UK, UAE, Europe, Australia, and beyond.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you export furniture internationally from Jodhpur? +

Yes, ScenicOne exports handcrafted furniture to over 50 countries worldwide, including the USA, UK, UAE, Australia, Europe, and Canada. We handle all export documentation and international shipping logistics from our Jodhpur workshop.

What types of wood do you use in your furniture? +

We primarily use Sheesham (Indian Rosewood), Teak, Mango Wood, and Acacia — all sustainably sourced solid hardwoods known for durability, beauty, and rich grain character. Each piece is crafted from hand-selected timber by our master craftsmen in Jodhpur.

Can I order custom-sized or custom-designed furniture? +

Absolutely. Custom orders are a core part of what we do. You can specify dimensions, wood type, finish colour, leg style, and upholstery. Our team will provide a detailed quote within 48 hours of your enquiry.

What is the minimum order for bulk or B2B orders? +

For hotel, restaurant, and commercial B2B orders, the minimum order value is typically ₹2,00,000 (approx. USD 2,400). For export container orders, we can fill 20-foot and 40-foot containers with mixed product selections.

How long does delivery take for international orders? +

Standard production time is 4–8 weeks depending on order size and customization. International sea freight from Jodhpur takes 4–6 additional weeks to European and American destinations, and 2–3 weeks to Middle Eastern destinations.

Do you supply furniture to hotels and restaurants? +

Yes, hospitality furniture is one of our specialities. We have furnished boutique hotels, resorts, restaurants, and cafés across India, the Middle East, Europe, and the USA. We provide custom hotel furniture packages including dining, bedroom, lobby, and outdoor furniture sets.

What warranty do you offer on your furniture? +

All ScenicOne furniture comes with a 2-year manufacturer warranty covering structural defects. Our solid wood pieces are built to last generations with proper care. We provide detailed care instructions with every order.
